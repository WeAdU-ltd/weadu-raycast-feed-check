# Raycast Store texts (English)

## Title

WeAdU Feed Check

## Description (package.json)

Check a pasted Google Shopping feed for missing title, GTIN, price, image, and link. Local only.

## Command

- Title: Check Shopping Feed
- Description: Audit a pasted Google Shopping feed

## Categories

Productivity, Data

## Long description (README excerpt)

Paste a Google Shopping feed (RSS, Atom, CSV, or tab-separated) and get the problems of each product: empty required fields, titles that are too long or too short, invalid GTIN check digits, prices that are zero or have no currency, and duplicate ids. Up to 500 products per check. Copy the findings of a product in one action.

The check runs on your computer. No network request, no sign-in, no telemetry. A product with no finding is not an approval from Google.

Free and open source (MIT), made by WeAdU.
