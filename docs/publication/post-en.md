Hi everyone, we just published **WeAdU Feed Check** in the Store: LINK

It checks a Google Shopping feed you paste (RSS, Atom, CSV, or TSV) and lists each product's problems: empty required fields, invalid GTIN check digits, prices of zero or without a currency, titles that are too long or too short, duplicate ids. Up to 500 products per check.

Everything runs locally: no network request, no sign-in. Free and MIT licensed.

Disclosure: I work at WeAdU, the agency that made it. Feedback welcome in this thread.
